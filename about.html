ABOUT

Technical work grounded in quantitative reasoning.

My work combines programming, statistics, modeling, and data analysis. I am currently pursuing an MSc in Computational Biology & Bioinformatics at ETH Zürich after completing a Computer Science degree with a minor in Mathematics at NYU.

I am particularly interested in problems where quantitative analysis can clarify a complex decision: evaluating models, extracting signal from noisy data, and communicating results in a way that is useful to technical and non-technical audiences.
